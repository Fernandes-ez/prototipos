## Brand & Style

This design system embraces an authentic, luminous Brazilian solar aesthetic tailored for a high-vibe tanning studio. It rejects heavy, opulent dark-gold tropes in favor of natural radiance, warm sand, glowing skin, sea salt, and midsummer freshness. The visual language evokes sun-warmed linen, beachside breeze, and effortless confidence.

The design movement combines **warm editorial minimalism** with **tactile sun-drenched realism**:
- Generous, airy negative space that breathes like an open-air coastline lounge.
- Subtle organic line-work reminiscent of sun-kissed bikini lines ("marquinha") used as accents and divider motifs.
- Natural textures and sun-lit clarity instead of synthetic SaaS cards, tech gradients, or artificial neon.
- Photography-forward art direction spotlighting real, dewy skin textures, natural sunlight shadows, and rhythmic waves.

## Colors

The palette directly mirrors a radiant coastal environment:

- **Primary (`#C46B47` Terracota Solar):** Earthy, sun-baked clay and golden hour warmth used for dominant call-to-actions, active elements, and core brand highlights. Supported by `#A25232` for pressed and hover states.
- **Secondary (`#D4A054` Âmbar Bronze):** Luminous solar gold reflecting treated, tanned skin; ideal for badges, glowing accents, and secondary actions, backed by `#B87D28` for depth.
- **Tertiary (`#2D7C8D` Azul Piscina / Céu de Verão):** A cool, refreshing aquatic counterbalance providing instant visual relief and contrast for status indicators, freshness tags, or beachside cues.
- **Neutral Dark (`#2C211B` Café Ébano):** Rich, deep organic espresso replacing sterile black to deliver high-contrast readability against pale sand surfaces. Secondary body text utilizes `#4A3B32`.
- **Backgrounds & Canvases (`#FBF7F2` Areia Clara & `#F5EFEB` Duna Quente):** Velvety, sunlit off-whites creating an inviting, tactile base without screen glare.
- **Tan-line Accent (`#FFF9F2` Linha Marquinha):** Ultra-pale sunline cream applied as crisp, delicate hairline borders (`1px`) over sun-kissed terracotta surfaces to evoke defined bikini lines.

## Typography

The typography unites bold, sunlit presence with effortless clarity:

- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Brings an expressive, sculptured rhythm with sculpted letterforms that feel organic, high-energy, and uniquely Brazilian. Headings set the tone with editorial flair, commanding attention without feeling corporate.
- **Body & Functional Copy (Outfit):** A clean, geometric yet human-centered sans-serif that remains highly legible across varying display densities, matching the laid-back beachside mood while ensuring seamless booking and reading experiences.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing:** Labels and tags leverage uppercase styling with tracking (+0.04em to +0.08em) for crisp legibility against sandy backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around spacious, open rhythm that mimics beachfront architecture—generous ventilation, relaxed margins, and structured modularity:

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max-width container), reflowing to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) and a 4-column layout on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm utilizing `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` between content modules ensures the interface feels warm, unhurried, and peaceful rather than packed like administrative software.
- **Reflow & Alignment:** Mobile views pin actionable booking controls to an ergonomic sticky bottom dock, freeing canvas area for immersive imagery and clear pricing clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through sunlight layering and physical texture, completely steering clear of heavy digital drop shadows:

- **Tonal Sand Surfaces:** Elevation is indicated by stepping from base sand (`#FBF7F2`) to warm dune cards (`#F5EFEB`), creating organic separation without artificial contrast jumps.
- **Solar Ambient Shadows:** Where elevation is necessary (such as sticky booking selectors or floating menus), use diffused amber-tinted ambient glows:
  - Subtle: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 33, 27, 0.05)`
  - Raised: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(196, 107, 71, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 33, 27, 0.04)`
- **The "Marquinha" Inset Border:** Active components and featured packages use a double-edge sensory effect: a gentle terracotta fill bordered by an ultra-thin `1px` high-contrast crisp sand outline (`#FFF9F2`), evoking the sharp, flawless tan line.
- **Water Shimmer (Backdrop Blur):** Translucent overlays use `rgba(251, 247, 242, 0.85)` with a `12px` blur to mimic light passing through water.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances coastal fluidity with clean modern contours:

- **Base Corner Radius:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for compact UI elements such as inputs, badges, and controls.
- **Large Containers & Cards:** `1rem` (`16px`) to produce soft, organic stone-like containers that feel tactile and welcoming.
- **Pill Accents:** Interactive buttons, booking triggers, and seasonal chips feature full rounded pill profiles (`9999px`), echoing surfboards, smooth sea stones, and gentle waves.
- **Organic Cutouts:** Occasional wave-carved arch profiles (`border-radius: 40px 40px 16px 16px`) on feature images evoke cabana entrances and sun arches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Terracota Solar):** Pill-shaped (`roundedness: full`), filled with `#C46B47`, text in `#FFF9F2` (`Syne` semibold). Hover shifts to `#A25232` with a subtle amber shadow.
- **Secondary (Bikini Line / Marquinha):** Transparent surface with a defined `1.5px` border in `#C46B47`, text in `#C46B47`. Hover transitions to `#F5EFEB` with a `#2C211B` text shift.
- **Refresh / Pool Action:** Pill-shaped filled with `#2D7C8D`, text in `#FFFFFF`, reserved for hydration, post-sun care tips, or live pool availability.

### Cards (Natural Studio Panels)
- Never use generic SaaS cards with grey borders. Use `#F5EFEB` solid backgrounds with an optional `1px` border in `#EADBCE`.
- Treatment & package cards feature a sun-glow hover: background warms slightly toward `#F3E9DF`, accompanied by an active `1.5px` terracotta accent border.

### Chips & Badges
- **Bronze Grade Chips:** Pill-shaped with subtle `#D4A054` (15% opacity) background, `#B87D28` text, and uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Freshness / Duration Chips:** Pill-shaped with `#2D7C8D` (12% opacity) background and `#2D7C8D` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FBF7F2` with a `1.5px` border in `#E0D2C3`.
- Active focus state: border shifts firmly to `#C46B47` accompanied by a soft `3px` glow of `rgba(196, 107, 71, 0.15)`. No cold blue rings.
- Text uses `#2C211B`, placeholder in warm sand `#968377`.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- Custom circular check controls matching the tanning mark silhouette. Unchecked: `1.5px` border in `#C46B47`. Checked: filled `#C46B47` with an internal sand dot (`#FFF9F2`).

### Studio Specials: "Marquinha" Visual Divider
- A custom horizontal divider: a micro-thin double line where a warm terracotta hairline sits directly adjacent to a bright sand highlight (`#FFF9F2`), creating the iconic sun-tan imprint aesthetic throughout long editorial scrolls.
