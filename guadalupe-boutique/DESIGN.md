## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the relaxed elegance of a warm, sun-drenched morning walk in the interior of São Paulo. Drawing inspiration from independent editorial fashion magazines (such as *The Gentlewoman* and *Holiday Magazine*), it translates classic tailoring and natural textiles into a digital lookbook. 

The aesthetic is tailored specifically for mature, discerning women who value longevity, texture, and understated luxury over transient trends. 

### Core Principles
- **Editorial Poise:** The interface presents collections through deliberate pacing, narrative storytelling, and editorial spreads rather than high-density utility grids.
- **Tactile Warmth:** Surfaces mimic textured linen, raw cotton, and sunlit limestone. No synthetic gloss, neon accents, or digital artifacts.
- **Restraint & Dignity:** Interactions are quiet. Traditional aggressive e-commerce heuristics—such as countdowns, badge clutter, star ratings, and floating checkout drawers—are replaced by personal styling consultation, lookbook narratives, and private atelier inquiries.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in sun-bleached natural fibers, deep roasted coffee, and soft botanical pigments.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#2A1810` / `#331D14`):** Dark roasted espresso. Used for primary typography, editorial rules, and structural elements. It replaces generic pitch black to maintain warm optical contrast against linen surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` & `#F5EFEB`):** Unbleached raw cotton and warm washed linen. Forms the alternating foundation of editorial spreads, sidebars, and full-bleed article backgrounds.
- **Accent Blush (`#C48B8B` / `#D9A5A0`):** Dusty botanical rose. Used with extreme restraint for editorial accents, seasonal curation markers, and subtle interactive states.
- **Support Botanical (`#8A9A86` / `#A3B19F`):** Muted sage leaf. Applied to artisanal notes, material sourcing tags, and collection provenance.
- **Support Linen & Borders (`#DFD3C3` / `#E6DCD1`):** Straw beige and hairline boundaries. Used exclusively for delicate `1px` structural division lines and passive containers.

## Typography

Typography establishes the cadence of an independent publication. 

- **Display & Headings:** `Bodoni Moda` provides high-contrast transitional elegance, celebrating classical editorial proportions with razor-thin serifs and dramatic vertical stress. It is set with relaxed line heights to let headlines breathe.
- **Narrative Quotes:** Editorial callouts and artisanal notes leverage `Bodoni Moda` in italicized regular weight, bringing intimacy to stories of tailoring and heritage.
- **Body & Utilities:** `Manrope` provides modern, geometric balance. Its open apertures and unpretentious character offer crisp legibility on small viewports without detracting from the serif headlines.
- **Micro-labels & Folios:** Small uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.18em`) transforms navigation items, catalog codes, and garment attributes into refined magazine folios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial 12-column asymmetric grid that privileges generous margins, dramatic vertical offsets, and intentional white space over uniform grids.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 64px (`margin`) outer margins and 32px (`gutter`) gutters. Allows asymmetric placement where images span 7 columns, offset captions span 3 columns, and 2 columns remain empty to create breathing space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 40px outer margins and 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 24px (`margin-mobile`) outer margins and 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Elements flow in a single editorial vertical scroll reminiscent of a pocket journal.

### Rhythm & Composition
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Adjacent imagery within lookbooks must alternate vertical alignment (`space-xl` margin tops on even items) to avoid commercial repetition.
- **Editorial Dividers:** Content sections are separated by structural hairline borders (`1px solid #E6DCD1`) spanning the full or partial column grid, accompanied by chapter folios (`label-editorial`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects artificial drop shadows, floating elevations, and layered card blurs. Depth is achieved exclusively through physical paper metaphors:

- **Surface Tiers:** Visual depth relies on flat, alternating planes of `#FAF7F2` (Base Canvas) and `#F5EFEB` (Secondary Well / Focus Panels).
- **Hairline Framing:** Crisp, single-pixel borders (`1px solid #E6DCD1`) delineate architectural boundaries, fitting rooms, and story capsules.
- **Overlapping Planes:** Subtle negative margins on high-resolution photography place imagery across structural lines, creating physical layering without software shadows.
- **Modal Backdrops:** Overlays for private appointments or fabric inspections utilize an opaque wash of `#2A1810` at 40% opacity, paired with an unrounded, solid `#FAF7F2` center canvas framed by a 1px border.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural and rectilinear (`roundedness: 0`). 

No rounded corners, soft bubbles, or pill shapes are permitted. All buttons, containers, imagery masks, and dialogs feature pristine, unchamfered 90-degree right angles. This echoes the precision of pattern cutting, high-end stationery, and museum catalog layouts.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action:** Sharp rectangular blocks filled with `#2A1810`, text set in `label-editorial` with crisp `#FAF7F2` uppercase typography. Height is fixed to 48px with generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`). Hover transition subtly shifts background to `#331D14`.
- **Editorial Text Links:** The predominant interaction pattern. Composed of `label-editorial` text with an offset underline (`1px solid #2A1810` positioned 4px beneath text). Hover state transitions underline color to `#C48B8B`.
- **Secondary Outlined:** Sharp borders (`1px solid #2A1810`) on a transparent background, retaining uppercase tracked labels.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Underline Input:** Minimal, single-border bottom line (`1px solid #DFD3C3`) directly under input text; no enclosing boxes. Focus state transitions the bottom border to `#2A1810`.
- **Floating Labels:** Small uppercase labels (`label-sm`) resting permanently above the baseline, colored in `#8A9A86` or `#2A1810`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted sharp square selectors (`14px x 14px`) outlined in `1px solid #2A1810`. Selected state is filled with an inner `#2A1810` square, avoiding generic system controls.

### Lookbook Tiles & Exhibition Cards
- **Image Containers:** Strictly rectilinear, featuring 3:4 and 4:5 vertical aspect ratios. Images rest directly on the surface without drop shadows or ambient glows.
- **Lookbook Captions:** Placed below or beside images using `caption` or `body-sm`. Accompanied by small Roman numeral indexing or fabric composition details in `label-sm`.
- **Price Presentation:** Stated quietly in `body-sm` beside the garment title, never accented in oversized, bright, or promotional colors.

### Atelier Consultations & Inquiries
- **Direct Concierge Action:** In place of a quick-add cart button, each piece features an "Agendar Prova no Atelier" (Book Fitting) or "Conversar com Estilista" (Consult Stylist) action link, prioritizing high-touch clienteling.
- **Fabric Detail Modules:** Secondary accordions framed by top and bottom `1px solid #E6DCD1` rules, expanding to reveal linen grain, weave origins, and care recommendations.
